## 2. Goals and scope

The goal is a minimal visitor-facing full-stack contact flow that collects a name and email, stores each valid submitted pair in the backend, and confirms only a successful submission.

- **[AFU-REQ-001](#provenance-AFU-REQ-001)** — The application has exactly three pages: Home, Contact Form, and Confirmation. The primary journey is Home to Contact Form to Confirmation after a successful submission; the return actions lead to Home.
- **[AFU-REQ-002](#provenance-AFU-REQ-002)** — Match the Figma design’s visual treatment, content, and shared patterns for the three pages, subject to the actual submitted pair shown on Confirmation (AFU-REQ-010). The design describes a Details header, pale neutral background, centered content panels/cards, green accents, page copy, icons, and shared footer (`design-description`, lines 9–62).
- **[AFU-REQ-003](#provenance-AFU-REQ-003)** — Do not add authentication, external integrations or third-party services, analytics, or additional pages. The internal frontend-to-backend submission and persistence are required parts of this application, not third-party integrations.

The pinned destination repositories are greenfield at the inspected revisions: frontend `https://github.com/demeedogg/frontend-test-app.git` `main@858555bf61e4c27218013d1d542bce07745fc16d` and backend `https://github.com/demeedogg/backend-test-app.git` `main@e32f25d9ddb0612877f778f00e2de22bf0e1107c` (`repositories`, lines 3–12; `analysis:frontend-test-app-dfaf2284`, lines 5–12; `analysis:backend-test-app-7cad18d3`, lines 5–12). No existing product behavior is evidenced in either checkout; the requested application remains to be implemented.

## 3. Actors, roles and permissions

- **[AFU-RULE-001](#provenance-AFU-RULE-001)** — The actor is an unauthenticated visitor. No sign-in, account, or authorization step is required to view the pages, enter the two fields, submit, or use the specified navigation. This follows the Work’s no-authentication constraint.
- **[AFU-RULE-002](#provenance-AFU-RULE-002)** — A visitor supplies their own name and email. The application submits that pair to its backend; no other recipient is specified. Backend storage and retention are described under AFU-REQ-006 and AFU-RULE-005.

The backend is an application component that processes and stores submissions, not a separate human role. No administrative UI or privileged actor is in scope.

## 4. Entities, data and states

- **[AFU-RULE-003](#provenance-AFU-RULE-003)** — A submission is the visitor-provided Name and Email pair. Both values are required; Name has a length from 1 through 100 characters, and Email must have valid email format and be no longer than 254 characters. “Valid format” does not mean mailbox ownership or deliverability; no email verification or email service is required.
- **[AFU-REQ-006](#provenance-AFU-REQ-006)** — For every valid submission accepted by the backend, store that submitted name/email pair. The backend’s storage outcome is the source of truth for whether the submission succeeded.
- **[AFU-RULE-004](#provenance-AFU-RULE-004)** — Repeated submissions, including identical name/email pairs, are allowed. Each accepted submission is a separate submission; equality with a prior pair is not a reason to reject it or suppress its storage.
- **[AFU-RULE-005](#provenance-AFU-RULE-005)** — Retain each stored pair indefinitely: no time-based expiry or scheduled deletion is part of the product behavior. This is the binding human clarification, not an inference from the design’s privacy reassurance. It does not add a manual deletion feature or promise immunity from physical storage failure.
- **[AFU-RULE-006](#provenance-AFU-RULE-006)** — The application imposes no request-frequency or total-record limit. A valid submission is not rejected by an application-level rate counter or record-count cap. This human mandate does not assert infinite physical capacity: if the backend cannot store a submission or otherwise reports failure, the existing backend-failure behavior applies (AFU-REQ-009). Do not implement a hidden application-level cap.

The visitor’s field values begin as unsaved form input. A submission is pending while the backend processes it. A successful backend response transitions the visitor to Confirmation and the stored pair is then summarized there (AFU-REQ-008 and AFU-REQ-010). A backend failure leaves the visitor on the form and displays the specified failure message (AFU-REQ-009). If persistence succeeded but the success response was not observed, the visitor may see failure despite a stored pair; retrying may store another pair. This is compatible with the duplicate rule, not a guarantee that a reported failure means no record exists (`work`, successful-response, backend-failure, and duplicate-submission clauses; `analysis:backend-test-app-7cad18d3`, lines 14–22).

## 5. Processes and functionality

### Navigate from Home to the form

The visitor opens Home and activates “Go to contact form” to reach Contact Form. The Home and Contact navigation and the return actions remain within the three in-scope pages (AFU-REQ-001).

- **[AFU-AC-001](#provenance-AFU-AC-001)** — The visitor can start at Home, reach Contact Form using its primary action, and return to Home from the form and Confirmation. The page count remains exactly three.

### Validate and submit contact details

The visitor enters Name and Email and activates “Submit details.” The field rules are AFU-RULE-003; errors appear below the corresponding invalid field under AFU-REQ-007. The design supplies the invalid-email example “Enter a valid email address, like alex@example.com.” (`design-description`, lines 37–39).

- **[AFU-AC-002](#provenance-AFU-AC-002)** — Missing values and values outside the specified field constraints are identified at their respective fields and are not submitted as valid data; boundary values of Name length 1 and 100 and Email length 254 are accepted when otherwise valid.

- **[AFU-REQ-007](#provenance-AFU-REQ-007)** — Show a field-specific validation error below each invalid Name or Email field. Use the design’s evidenced invalid-email message where applicable; concise field- and criterion-specific copy for the unspecified required-field and Name errors is a minor presentation detail.

The Work requires field-specific validation but does not specify exact copy for missing fields or invalid Name values. As a minor presentation detail, use concise field- and criterion-specific text for those cases; use the design’s evidenced copy for the illustrated invalid-email state. Validate when the visitor submits, and clear a field’s error when its value is corrected or revalidated. These choices do not change the validation rules or page flow (`work`, validation and minor-detail clauses; `design-description`, lines 37–39, 64–66).

For valid values, the frontend submits the pair to the backend.

- **[AFU-AC-003](#provenance-AFU-AC-003)** — On successful storage, the pair is present in backend data; repeating an identical valid submission is accepted and produces a separate stored submission. Each stored pair remains without time-based expiry.
- **[AFU-AC-004](#provenance-AFU-AC-004)** — Submission frequency or the current record count alone never triggers an application-level rejection. This does not override a genuine storage/backend failure due to finite physical capacity or service unavailability.

### Resolve submission outcome

- **[AFU-REQ-008](#provenance-AFU-REQ-008)** — Show Confirmation only after a successful backend response indicating successful processing/storage. A pending request or an unsuccessful response is not confirmation.
- **[AFU-REQ-009](#provenance-AFU-REQ-009)** — On backend failure, remain on Contact Form and show exactly: “Unable to submit your information. Please try again.” No separate limit-specific screen or message is added. A reported failure may be followed by a retry; because duplicate submissions are accepted, a retry after an ambiguous post-storage failure can create an additional stored pair.
- **[AFU-REQ-010](#provenance-AFU-REQ-010)** — On Confirmation, show the actual submitted Name and Email pair in the Figma summary treatment, not the example values. As a minor content detail, replace the sample first name in the thank-you sentence with the submitter’s first name while preserving the sentence’s meaning and styling.
- **[AFU-AC-005](#provenance-AFU-AC-005)** — A successful backend outcome leads to Confirmation and displays the submitted pair; a failure remains on Contact Form with the exact specified message. A visitor can correct or retry from the form, but the failure message alone does not assert that no record was stored.

The exact three-page scope, no-authentication and no-external-integration constraints, field validation, duplicate acceptance, success/failure behavior, indefinite retention, and no-application-level-volume-cap behavior are binding (AFU-REQ-001, AFU-REQ-003, AFU-RULE-003 through AFU-RULE-006, AFU-REQ-008, and AFU-REQ-009).
